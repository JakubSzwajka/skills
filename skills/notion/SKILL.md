# Notion

- Use the authenticated `ntn` CLI for Notion interactions.
- Always load and apply the `unslop` skill before writing or editing Notion content.
- For specs and tickets, use `to-spec` or `to-tickets` instead of drafting them directly.
- Apply `technical-writing` when the content is technical documentation, reference, explanation, or procedure.
- Put only information that gives a later reader something useful to understand, decide, or do.
- Never remove, archive, replace, or materially shorten existing content without the user's explicit approval. Treat approval as limited to the named page or content.
